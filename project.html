Current project

Ten Minutes

A real-time AI voice tarot consultation. Talk about a concern, choose cards, and explore what they mean with Lena.

Working prototype / integration testing

Product direction

Ten Minutes explores a low-friction way to discuss everyday concerns by voice. The first product emphasizes relationship questions while allowing open-ended consultation.

The experience is entertainment and reflection, not professional advice or a prediction of certain outcomes.

Meet Lena

Lena is a confident, candid and playful tarot reader. She listens without forcing agreement, uses natural Korean speech, and encourages the user to make their own decisions.

The Queen mindset evolved from a bold character concept into a more nuanced balance of directness, warmth and independence.

User journey

The mobile experience combines voice conversation with direct visual card selection. The user explains a concern, selects three cards and hears each interpretation before continuing the conversation.

StepInteraction
01 StartEnter the service and start a voice session.
02 ClarifyDiscuss the concern and establish the question.
03 ChooseSelect three cards directly on screen.
04 ReadReveal and interpret each card in sequence.
05 ContinueDiscuss follow-up questions and possible next steps.
06 ReportClose the time-bounded session and review the personal result.

Four three-card spreads

SpreadPositions
Time progressionPast / present / possible future
Situation and adviceSituation / obstacle / advice
RelationshipMe / other person / relationship
Choice comparisonOption A / option B / advice

Visual identity and deck

  • The team explored Y2K, metallic and combined visual concepts and selected a metallic direction.
  • The design includes a character identity, a mobile call interface, individual card reveal and a personal report.
  • The deck contains 78 illustrations: 22 major and 56 minor arcana. The web assets are optimized and mapped to stable card identifiers.
