Current project

Voice tarot

Ten Minute is the leading name for the voice tarot service. The team is still considering whether to include tarot in the name and whether to use Korean.

Product direction

Focus on relationship questions while keeping open-ended voice consultations. Offer suggested topics as an optional starting point. The model classifies the concern from the conversation.

The reader has a cool, direct style inspired by Lee Hyori. Give brief, situation-specific feedback instead of long card explanations. Test whether prompt instructions can make the conversation feel faster.

Report and spreads

Provide the model with four three-card spreads and ask it to choose the best fit: past, present, future; situation, obstacle, advice; me, the other person, relationship; or option A, option B, advice.

Reveal cards one at a time. The personal report includes card images, consultation date and time, a short message from the reader, and a one-line situation summary.

Sharing

Share a URL with a compelling, light topic and a thumbnail, such as a question about finding a partner by Christmas. The destination includes a Try it myself button.

The full personal report remains private. Share content centers on the hook rather than sensitive consultation details. Masking is excluded because of implementation complexity. Preselected topics are the preferred viral format.

Development and next work

Align card draws with spoken interpretation. Consider a separate backend agent or model for card tasks. Check voice pacing and API capabilities during implementation.

Create a cover image with the name, design and implement the report, and generate 78 card images. Deploy frontend and backend projects on Vercel, separating database and authentication from the Realtime API relay. Purchase and connect a domain.

Each member will improve and test prompts based on SOUL.md, write example conversations by relationship scenario, and try comparable phone consultation services and share feedback. Record the experience if possible.
