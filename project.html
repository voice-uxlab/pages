Current project

Voice tarot

The team changed MIND DETOXING to voice tarot at the third meetup. The final product name remains open.

Research question

A playful conversation through voice

The user talks about an everyday question, selects a card, and hears an interpretation. The AI reader responds to follow-up questions.

Direction under discussion

The first version will use a mobile website with an immediate voice connection. Scheduled calls, payments, and additional characters remain later options.

A working product and live voice test results are not yet available. The team will first do tests of one complete user flow.

The success criterion is a working product and a successful December 16 demo. Sona is an optional tool supplied by the leader.
