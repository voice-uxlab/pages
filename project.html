Selected project

MIND DETOXING

The team selected MIND DETOXING at the second meetup. It is a voice service for everyday reflection. The product scope is not final.

Research question

Everyday reflection through voice

The team will examine whether speaking freely and reviewing an organized summary helps users gain distance from their thoughts and notice recurring patterns.

Direction under discussion

The overtime discussion explored hands-free thought dumping, private time and space, trust, and personal pattern checklists. These are ideas to test, not completed functions.

The team must define the initial users, service boundaries, minimum complete experience, roles, and milestones. No user test results are available.
