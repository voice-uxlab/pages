About Voice UX Lab

We built a voice-first prototype during this eight-week program. This archive documents the work and our learning.

The program

Voice UX Lab is part of OpenAI × IT’s Study, cohort 5. Team members must give approval for their public profiles.

The program runs from October 3 to November 30, 2026. Meetings are in person near Yangjae Citizen’s Forest Station. Weeks 1 and 2 ran from 14:00 to 18:00 KST. Weeks 3–8 run from 14:00 to 17:00 KST.

This website contains the team’s work. It is not an official OpenAI website.

The final regular meeting is planned for November 29; the official activity period ends November 30. The cohort presentation is scheduled for December 16.

Work archive

Public records include reviewed meeting summaries and asynchronous project milestones.

The team will publish only approved content. Private team storage contains restricted materials, raw recordings, contact details, attendance data, and consent records.

Voice as an interface

The team explored real-time speech, application-controlled actions and the balance between voice and screen interaction.

The agent must identify the task and keep information from the conversation. At the third meetup, the team selected voice tarot.

Tools & preparation

The project uses OpenAI Realtime, Typecast, React, FastAPI and PostgreSQL. Codex supported collaborative development.

Shared learning

IT’s Study is an AI study network. Team members examine technical questions and do exercises together.

Team members explored different workstreams and learned from collaborative design and software development.
