About Voice UX Lab

We will get knowledge about voice interfaces and make an AI agent. We will keep records of our work.

The program

Voice UX Lab is part of OpenAI × IT’s Study, cohort 5. Team members must give approval for their public profiles.

The program period is October 3 to November 30, 2026. The team plan gives Sunday meetings from 14:00 to 17:00 KST. The meeting area is near Yangjae Citizen’s Forest Station. The team uses Google Meet for online meetings. The team must give each date and the meeting location.

This website contains the team’s work. It is not an official OpenAI website.

Work archive

Weekly records contain our work and the reasons for our decisions. The final report will contain the design, demo, results, and team members’ comments.

The team will publish only approved content. Private team storage contains restricted materials, raw recordings, contact details, attendance data, and consent records.

Voice as an interface

The team will make a voice interface for tasks that use a screen and keyboard. The study includes real-time speech, voice interfaces, and tool calls.

The agent must identify requests and keep the conversation context. It must use tools and respond when requests change. Sona is a proposed concept. The team will select the project topic in week 3.

Tools & preparation

The study uses the OpenAI Realtime API and Codex. Use a laptop with a microphone, speakers, and Wi-Fi. Make sure you have OpenAI, GitHub, and Notion accounts. Experience with Codex and ChatGPT Voice Mode can help.

Shared learning

IT’s Study is a network for people who get knowledge about AI. Members give information and help each other get skills.

Activity records will contain new knowledge, tools, questions, and discussion results. They will also contain preparation tasks for the next meeting.
