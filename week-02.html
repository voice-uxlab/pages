Overtime discussion: everyday reflection

After the meetup, a few members explored a lighter role than specialized mental healthcare. These ideas did not finalize the product scope.

Service role: everyday reflection

The team explored help with expressing and organizing everyday thoughts, with a lighter role than specialized mental healthcare.

Speak, organize, gain distance, notice patterns

  • A hands-free brain dump could let users speak freely instead of typing, then review an organized account of their day.
  • Distance from their thoughts may help users view situations more objectively and notice recurring triggers or patterns. A personal checklist was suggested.

Conditions for use and trust

  • Users need private time and a space where they can speak comfortably. Some users value writing by hand, so the team must examine when speech is useful.
  • People may share worries with a trusted listener who helps them notice assumptions, biases, mistaken judgments, or leaps in reasoning. A diary or spoken account can still be selective or incomplete.
