2026-11-01 / 14:00–17:00 KST

Fourth regular meetup: voice tarot flow and character

Venue
Hyundai Motor and Kia headquarters, Yangjae

The team defined the mobile voice tarot flow and selected a Y2K Queen character direction. Screen drafts and an exploratory voice test informed the next development tasks. The complete service is not yet verified.

Decisions from the meeting

  • Start with a mobile website and one reader. Users start an immediate voice connection.
  • Use social login, an editable nickname, a reader profile, and a call button. Naver and Kakao are the initial login candidates.
  • For the first implementation, users speak three card numbers from a 78-card deck. Add a visual draw effect if feasible.
  • Provide mute and end controls. After the call, show the selected cards and a generated conversation summary.
  • Develop a cheerful, confident Queen character with a Y2K visual direction. Use Python for the backend.

Observations and open questions

  • In an exploratory voice test, the AI said it had selected cards rather than drawn them at random. It also inferred personal context without sufficient user information.
  • Define the random draw procedure, number recognition, interruption behavior, and session ending. Korean voice quality and session cost still need tests.
  • Time-limited prepaid access was discussed, including 10-minute passes. Prices, free trials, extensions, and unused time remain open.
  • The final product name, voice provider, storage policy, and hosting configuration remain open. OpenAI login and subscription-based API cost savings are unverified ideas.

Next work: November 8

  • The character and planning group will prepare a mood board and multi-turn dialogue examples for different concerns.
  • The frontend group will share editable screen drafts and build an initial prototype with a real voice API connection.
  • The backend group will document the technology stack, authentication, account management, data, database, and infrastructure.
  • Review the visual consistency after the mood board is selected. Confirm task owners and shared development rules.
  • At the next meeting, review the character materials, live voice prototype, and backend specification together.
