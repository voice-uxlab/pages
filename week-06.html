2026-11-15 / 14:00–17:00 KST

Sixth regular meetup: report, sharing, and voice persona

Venue
Hyundai Motor and Kia headquarters, Yangjae

The team discussed the report, sharing, the reader persona, and a focus on relationship questions. The work below remains planned unless stated otherwise.

Product direction

  • Ten Minute is the leading name. Whether to include tarot or use Korean remains open. Focus on relationship questions while keeping free consultation; suggested topics are optional, and the model classifies concerns from the conversation.

Report and spreads

  • Provide four spreads to the model and ask it to select one: past, present, future; situation, obstacle, advice; me, the other person, relationship; or option A, option B, advice.
  • Reveal the cards one at a time. The personal report includes card images, consultation date and time, a short message from the reader, and a one-line situation summary.

Sharing

  • Share a URL with a light, compelling thumbnail and a Try it myself button on the destination. The full personal report stays private; share images focus on a hook. Masking is excluded due to complexity, and preselected topics are preferred for viral sharing.

Persona and voice

  • Use a cool, direct style inspired by Lee Hyori. Give short feedback suited to the situation rather than long card explanations. Test prompt instructions for a faster conversational tempo.
  • The team observed a timing mismatch between drawing a card and interpreting it. Consider a separate backend agent or model for card tasks. The meeting compared Live API and Realtime API speed controls; verify capabilities during implementation.

Next work

  • Create a cover with the name and 78 card images. Design and implement the report.
  • Deploy frontend and backend projects on Vercel. Separate the database and authentication server from the Realtime API relay. Buy and connect a domain.
  • Each member will improve and test prompts based on SOUL.md, write few-shot examples by relationship scenario, and try comparable phone consultation services and share feedback. Record the experience if possible.
